# Promotion Policy — Workforce Intelligence Inc.

## 1. Purpose
This policy establishes fair and transparent criteria for employee promotions.

## 2. Eligibility Criteria
- Minimum 18 months in current role
- Performance rating of "Fully Meets" or above for the last 2 review cycles
- Completion of all mandatory training
- No active disciplinary actions
- Demonstrated readiness for increased responsibility

## 3. Promotion Types

### 3.1 Vertical Promotion
- Movement to a higher grade/level within the same function
- Salary increase of 10-20% based on the new grade band
- Updated title and expanded responsibilities

### 3.2 Lateral Promotion
- Movement to a similar level in a different department/function
- Focused on career development and skill broadening
- Salary adjustment as per new role band

## 4. Promotion Process
1. Manager nominates employee during the annual promotion cycle (Q4)
2. HR reviews eligibility criteria
3. Department head and skip-level manager approval
4. Calibration session with leadership team
5. Final approval by VP/Director
6. Promotion effective from January 1st of the following year

## 5. Assessment Criteria (Weighted)
- Performance Rating: 30%
- KPI Achievement: 25%
- Skill Development: 15%
- Leadership Potential: 15%
- Peer and 360 Feedback: 10%
- Tenure and Experience: 5%

## 6. Communication
- Promotion decisions communicated by direct manager within 2 weeks of approval
- Formal letter from HR with updated terms
- Updated in HRMS within 5 working days

## 7. Appeals
- Employees may appeal promotion decisions within 30 days
- Appeals reviewed by HR and an independent panel
