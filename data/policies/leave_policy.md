# Leave Policy — Workforce Intelligence Inc.

## 1. Purpose
This policy outlines the leave entitlements and procedures for all employees of Workforce Intelligence Inc.

## 2. Types of Leave

### 2.1 Annual Leave (Paid)
- Full-time employees: 20 working days per calendar year
- Part-time employees: Pro-rated based on working hours
- Accrual: 1.67 days per month of service
- Maximum carry-forward: 5 days to the next calendar year

### 2.2 Sick Leave
- 12 days per calendar year (non-cumulative)
- Medical certificate required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days
- Extended sick leave may be granted at management discretion

### 2.3 Personal Leave
- 5 days per year for personal matters
- Must be approved by direct manager at least 48 hours in advance
- Cannot be carried forward

### 2.4 Maternity Leave
- 26 weeks of paid leave for birth mothers
- 2 weeks of paid leave for partners/co-parents
- Applies after 80 days of continuous employment

### 2.5 Bereavement Leave
- 5 days for immediate family members
- 3 days for extended family members

## 3. Leave Application Process
1. Submit leave request through the HRMS portal at least 5 working days before planned leave
2. Manager approval required within 48 hours
3. Emergency leave may be reported within 24 hours of absence
4. HR department maintains all leave records

## 4. Public Holidays
- Employees are entitled to all national public holidays as declared annually
- If required to work on a public holiday, compensatory leave will be granted

## 5. Unauthorized Absence
- Unapproved absence may result in salary deduction
- Repeated unauthorized absence may lead to disciplinary action
