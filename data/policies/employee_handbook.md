# Employee Handbook — Workforce Intelligence Inc.

## 1. Welcome
Welcome to Workforce Intelligence Inc. This handbook provides essential information about company policies, culture, and expectations.

## 2. Company Values
- **Innovation**: We embrace AI, data science, and emerging technologies
- **Integrity**: We act ethically and transparently in all dealings
- **Collaboration**: We work across teams and departments to achieve common goals
- **Excellence**: We strive for the highest quality in everything we do
- **People First**: Our employees are our greatest asset

## 3. Employment Types
- **Full-Time**: 40 hours/week, eligible for all benefits
- **Part-Time**: Less than 30 hours/week, pro-rated benefits
- **Contract**: Fixed-term engagement, benefits as per contract terms

## 4. Working Hours
- Standard: Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM
- Lunch break: 1 hour (12:30 PM - 1:30 PM)
- Flexible hours available with manager approval

## 5. Compensation
- Monthly salary paid on the last working day
- Annual salary review in March/April
- Performance bonus: 0-20% of annual salary based on company and individual performance
- Stock options available for eligible employees

## 6. Benefits
- Health Insurance: Comprehensive medical, dental, and vision coverage for employee and dependents
- Life Insurance: 2x annual salary
- Retirement: 401(k) with 4% company match
- Employee Assistance Program (EAP)
- Gym membership subsidy: $50/month
- Annual health checkup

## 7. Code of Conduct
- Professional behavior expected at all times
- Zero tolerance for harassment and discrimination
- Conflicts of interest must be disclosed
- Confidentiality of company information must be maintained
- Social media usage policy applies

## 8. Performance Reviews
- Semi-annual performance reviews (June and December)
- Continuous feedback encouraged throughout the year
- Self-assessment, manager assessment, and peer feedback components
- Performance Improvement Plans (PIP) for underperforming employees
- PIP duration: 60-90 days with clear, measurable targets

## 9. Grievance Procedure
1. Raise concern with direct manager
2. If unresolved, escalate to HR department
3. Formal grievance submitted in writing
4. Investigation within 10 working days
5. Resolution communicated within 5 working days of investigation completion
6. Appeal process available within 15 days

## 10. Exit Process
- Minimum notice period: 30 days (60 days for senior management)
- Exit interview conducted by HR
- Knowledge transfer required
- All company property to be returned on last working day
- Final settlement within 45 days of last working day
