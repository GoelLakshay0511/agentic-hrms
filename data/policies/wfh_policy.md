# Work From Home Policy — Workforce Intelligence Inc.

## 1. Purpose
This policy provides guidelines for remote work and work-from-home arrangements.

## 2. Eligibility
- All full-time employees who have completed 3 months of service
- Role must be suitable for remote work (as determined by department head)
- Employees must have reliable internet connectivity and a suitable workspace
- New employees in their probation period may have limited WFH access

## 3. WFH Models

### 3.1 Hybrid Model (Default)
- 3 days in office, 2 days work from home per week
- Core in-office days: Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday (department-specific)
- WFH days: Monday and Friday (flexible)

### 3.2 Fully Remote
- Available for select roles with VP approval
- Quarterly in-office attendance required for team events
- Must be within the same time zone or +/- 2 hours

### 3.3 Flexible WFH
- Ad-hoc WFH requests approved by direct manager
- Maximum 5 ad-hoc WFH days per month

## 4. Working Hours
- Standard working hours apply regardless of location
- Core hours: 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM (must be available for meetings)
- Flexible start/end within the 8-hour workday
- Overtime policy applies equally to in-office and remote work

## 5. Equipment and Support
- Company provides laptop and essential peripherals
- Monthly internet allowance: $50 for hybrid, $100 for fully remote
- Ergonomic equipment available for request through IT

## 6. Communication Expectations
- Respond to messages within 1 hour during core hours
- Camera on during team meetings
- Daily check-in with manager (format flexible)
- Weekly team sync required

## 7. Data Security
- Use company VPN at all times
- No use of public WiFi for work without VPN
- Screen lock when away from workstation
- No company data on personal devices without MDM enrollment

## 8. Performance
- Same performance standards apply regardless of work location
- Regular 1:1 meetings with manager at least biweekly
- WFH privileges may be revoked if performance drops below expectations
