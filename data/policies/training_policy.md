# Training & Development Policy — Workforce Intelligence Inc.

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines the framework for employee training, skill development, and continuous learning.

## 2. Training Categories

### 2.1 Mandatory Training
- Compliance and Ethics (annual)
- Data Security Awareness (annual)
- Workplace Safety (upon joining and annually)
- Anti-Harassment Training (annual)

### 2.2 Professional Development
- Role-specific technical training
- Leadership development programs
- Industry certification preparation
- Conference attendance

### 2.3 Skill Enhancement
- Online learning platform access (LinkedIn Learning, Coursera, Udemy Business)
- Internal knowledge-sharing sessions
- Cross-functional rotation programs
- Mentorship programs

## 3. Training Budget
- Each employee is allocated up to $2,000 per year for external training
- Department heads may request additional budget for specialized programs
- Training costs include course fees, materials, travel, and accommodation

## 4. Training Hours
- Minimum 40 hours of training per year per employee
- Training time counts as regular working hours
- Employees must complete mandatory training within specified deadlines

## 5. Training Process
1. Employee identifies training need with manager during performance reviews
2. Submit Training Request Form through HRMS
3. Manager and HR approval required
4. Upon completion, employee submits certificate/proof
5. Training record updated in employee profile

## 6. Training Outcomes
- Training effectiveness evaluated through:
  - Post-training assessment
  - Performance improvement tracking
  - Manager feedback (90 days post-training)
  - Skill application on projects

## 7. Bond / Service Agreement
- For training exceeding $5,000, a 12-month service agreement may be required
- Early departure may require partial reimbursement
